    THE SKY IS JUST THE BEGINNING

    A leap for every letter.

    1. Pick your letters and pace, then start your climb.
    2. Type the letter on the glowing platform, or tap its key below.
    3. Make 100 leaps to reach the moon, then type 50 short words to reach the stars. Two-letter words come first; the final 30 leaps use three-letter words from the whole keyboard. A mistake resets your streak, but never takes away a leap.

    Relaxed mode lights up the next key. Timed modes give you 8 or 4 seconds per letter in the target. A timeout counts as a miss and gives you a fresh letter or word. Correct letters stay highlighted if you press a wrong key. Press Escape to pause.
